## Claim-by-Claim Audit

### Claim 1: Click-Through Rate +18%
**Quote:** "Öneri motoru tıklama oranını %18 artırdı. (Ekip, kendi seçtiği 2 günlük dönemde ölçtü; karşılaştırma dönemi yok.)"

**Evidence Available:** 2-day measurement window selected by team; no baseline period; no control group  
**Tier:** `[Y]` Misleading / `[H]` Plausible Hypothesis

**Justification:** The 2-day window was chosen AFTER seeing results. No comparison period exists. Selecting a favorable time window after launch and reporting the result as evidence is the textbook HARKing pattern. Without a baseline or control arm, this cannot separate engine effect from novelty or timing. The percentag itself may be true within that window, but the framing "the engine increased CTR" is not supported.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### Claim 2: Basket Value +3.1% (95% CI: 1.8–4.4)
**Quote:** "60.000 kullanıcı rastgele iki kola atandı; önceden kayıtlı birincil metrik olan sepet değeri yeni motorda %3,1 yüksek (%95 GA: 1,8–4,4). Analizi bağımsız veri ekibi yaptı."

**Evidence Available:**  
- Randomized assignment (60,000 users across two arms)  
- Pre-registered primary metric (stated before results seen)  
- Independent analysis team  
- Confidence interval excludes zero  

**Tier:** `[K]` Proven / Kanıtlanmış

**Justification:** Randomization eliminates confounding; pre-registration blocks HARKing on threshold; independent analysis rules out motivated reasoning in computation; CI excludes zero, meeting standard statistical threshold. This is the only claim in the set meeting the evidence structure required for tier-K.

**Verdict:** PROVEN

---

### Claim 3: Users Love the Engine
**Quote:** "Kullanıcılar yeni motoru seviyor. (Ürün ekibinin 12 kişilik iç testinde herkes olumlu görüş bildirdi.)"

**Evidence Available:** 12-person internal product team test; all positive feedback  
**Tier:** `[H]` Plausible Hypothesis

**Justification:** Sample size is tiny (n=12); sample is biased (product team members, not real users); no measurement instrument defined ("love" is interpretation, not a metric); no comparison to control condition; results of internal testers is not evidence of user behavior at scale. The absence of negative feedback in a 12-person team is expected when those 12 people have committed to the project.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### Claim 4: Better Than Competitors
**Quote:** "Motor, rakiplerden daha iyi öneri üretiyor. (Kaynak: tedarikçinin kendi benchmark'ı.)"

**Evidence Available:** Benchmark run by the supplier (the vendor of the engine)  
**Tier:** `[S]` Speculative / `[Y]` Misleading

**Justification:** Supplier-run benchmark on supplier's own product is a maximum-conflict-of-interest comparison. Suppliers optimize benchmarks for their products; they do not independently test. Without independent verification, this is not evidence, it is marketing. A vendor claiming their product is better than competitors' products on their own test meets zero audit standards for tier-K or even tier-H on this basis alone.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### Claim 5: Monthly Server Cost 42,000 TL
**Quote:** "Motorun sunucu maliyeti ayda 42.000 TL. (Denetlenmiş bulut faturası.)"

**Evidence Available:** Audited cloud invoice  
**Tier:** `[K]` Proven / Kanıtlanmış

**Justification:** This is a factual claim tied to a single audited invoice. The cost on a given month is verifiable and verified. No mechanism for error: a cost is a cost.

**Verdict:** PROVEN

---

### Claim 6: Reduced Churn
**Quote:** "Motor churn'ü azalttı. (Lansmandan sonraki ay churn düştü; aynı ay fiyat değişikliği de yapıldı.)"

**Evidence Available:** Month-over-month churn decline; price change also occurred in same month  
**Tier:** `[KKE]` Critical Control Missing / Kritik Kontrol Eksik

**Justification:** Churn dropped, but causation is unidentified. The same month saw a price change. Either the engine, the price change, or both could have driven the decline — or neither (seasonal, competitive shift, other product changes). To promote this to tier-K, a confound control is required: hold price constant and re-measure, or measure the engine impact on users in the cohort before the price change (if data exists). As stated, this is a "surprising positive" that flatters the hypothesis and therefore requires the symmetric control described in Registry mode procedure step 5. The control has not run.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

## Gap Map: Missing Elements

1. **No comparison period for CTR claim** — cannot separate novelty effect from sustained impact  
2. **No independent competitor benchmark** — claim 4 rests entirely on vendor self-assessment  
3. **No qualitative/quantitative user feedback at scale** — claim 3 based on 12-person internal test only  
4. **No confound control on price change** — churn decline has two possible causes  
5. **No mention of null hypothesis or kill condition** — no statement of what would cause the engine to be rejected  

---

## HARKing Status Statement

The claims were compiled AFTER launch and measurement. The team selected the time window for claim 1; no pre-specification of the basket-value metric is mentioned for claims 1, 3, 4, 6. The set is therefore entirely retrospective. Claim 2 explicitly states pre-registration and is the only exception. Claims 1, 3, 4, 6 carry implicit HARKing risk: favorable results may be those presented, not those originally expected.

---

## Recommendation

**Do not present these claims to the board as "proven."** The team lead's confidence is unfounded on four of six claims. The two tier-K claims (basket value, server cost) are solid and should be highlighted. For the board:

- ✓ Claim 2: Basket value up 3.1% (pre-registered, randomized, independent analysis) — **SAFE TO CLAIM**
- ✓ Claim 5: Monthly cost 42,000 TL (invoiced, audited) — **SAFE TO CITE** (as cost, not as value judgment)
- ✗ Claim 1: 18% CTR (cherry-picked window, no control) — **REMOVE or reframe as "2-day pilot result, not statistically validated"**
- ✗ Claim 3: "Users love it" (12-person internal test) — **REMOVE or reframe as "internal feedback positive; user research at scale pending"**
- ✗ Claim 4: "Better than competitors" (vendor benchmark) — **REMOVE entirely** (credibility damage risk is high)
- ✗ Claim 6: "Reduced churn" (confounded with price change) — **REMOVE or add: "Churn declined; price also changed same month; engine attribution TBD pending confound control"**

**Criticality:** HIGH. Presenting unvalidated claims as "proven" to the board creates legal and reputational risk if the engine underperforms or the claims are later audited independently.
