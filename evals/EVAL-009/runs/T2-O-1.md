**Audit of the retrofit board summary (retrospective, HARKing status: the claims were selected and written after the outcomes were known)**

The team lead's confidence is not supported by the document. Two of six claims are proven as stated. Two are not testable from what is written, and two are overreaches.

**1. Portfolio-wide energy savings: [Y] (misleading), NOT PROVEN.**
The only measurement is from one building the facilities team chose to showcase. That is a selection effect. Nothing in the document covers the other buildings. The claim should read "saved energy in the showcase building."

**2. Heat pumps quieter than old boilers: [S] (speculative), NOT PROVEN.**
The only source is the installer, who has a financial interest in the result. No sound measurement is cited. The claim is untested.

**3. 18% less gas per degree-day vs. prior two winters; matched control +1%: [K] for this building and period, PROVEN within scope.**
Meter bills are a direct instrument, and the non-retrofitted control on the same campus is a real confound check. Retrofit minus control is roughly −19 points, which is the kind of positive that needs a control, and one exists. Limits to state on the slide: one building and one control (n=1 pair, so no estimate of spread), a two-winter baseline (no check that the two buildings trended together before the retrofit), and the threshold was not preregistered. Before presenting, confirm that the control's figures were degree-day normalized the same way as the retrofitted building's.

**4. Tenants more comfortable: [S], NOT PROVEN.**
Complaints were not tracked before the retrofit, so there is no baseline and the claim cannot be tested. A drop in complaints after the retrofit would not prove comfort either, since complaint rates depend on reporting behaviour. The claim should be removed or replaced with "not measured; tracking starts now."

**5. Total installed cost £/€2.4 million: [K], PROVEN as stated.**
Source is the signed final invoice, reconciled by finance. Scope caveat: this is the contractor's invoiced total. If "total installed cost" is meant to include design fees, owner costs, or grants netted off, those are not shown here and the label should say so.

**6. Payback under 5 years: [Y] (misleading), NOT PROVEN.**
The calculation assumes gas stays at this winter's peak price. That is the most favourable input for savings, so it gives the shortest payback possible. The document does not state the annual £ savings or the gas price baseline, so the number cannot be reproduced. Also, the 18% figure from claim 3 is a single-building result. It should be re-run at a range of prices (for example, the 3-year average and a lower-price scenario) and the sensitivity shown.
