**1. Portfolio-wide savings `[Y]` / `[S]`.** The evidence is one building, chosen by the facilities team for its showcase value. That is a selection-biased sample of size one. Nothing in the summary measures any other building. Presenting it as "across the whole portfolio" frames a single case as a population result. *Next step:* rewrite as "one showcase building saved about 19% relative to its matched control." Extend to the portfolio only after a pre-specified sample of buildings is measured.

**2. Heat pumps quieter `[S]`.** The only source is the installer, who is interested in the sale. There is no measurement, no instrument, and no comparison. *Next step:* drop it, or measure dB(A) at fixed distances before and after with the same meter, and report it as a measurement.

**3. The 18% gas reduction `[K]` (narrow scope).** This is the strongest claim. It uses utility meter data, a matched control building on the same campus, and the same period. The difference-in-differences is roughly 18% − 1% ≈ 19% relative reduction, and the control is the symmetric check a surprising positive needs. Caveats the board should hear:
- It is one building against one control, so there is no measure of variance across buildings or seasons.
- The threshold was not locked before the data was seen. This is retrospective.
- The degree-day normalisation method and the matching criteria are not stated. Confirm both.
- Check whether occupancy, use, or thermostat settings changed in either building.

Before the board: confirm the 18% and 1% figures against the bills, and confirm the building is the same one used in claim 1.

**4. Tenant comfort `[S]` / `[KKE]`.** No complaints were tracked before the retrofit, so there is no baseline. Post-retrofit complaint counts alone cannot show a change. *Next step:* remove it, or start a comfort survey now and run it before and after the next heating cycle.

**5. Installed cost 2.4M `[K]`.** A signed final invoice, reconciled by finance, is a strong source. The scope of "total installed cost" is unstated. Confirm whether it includes VAT, professional fees, internal labour, and any grants netted off. Those cannot be checked from the summary.

**6. Payback under 5 years `[S]`.** No calculation is shown. Payback depends on annual savings, and the summary gives no baseline gas spend, so the figure cannot be reproduced. The assumption of peak gas prices is a choice that flatters the result. It is not a neutral input. Heat pump electricity costs and maintenance are not mentioned. *Next step:* show the calculation with annual gas spend, the 18% saving, a base-case price and a sensitivity range, and the electricity and maintenance costs.

**Suggested board framing:** Claims 3 and 5 are supported. Claim 3 should be stated for one building, with its control. Claim 1 should be reworded as a showcase result. Claims 2, 4 and 6 should be removed or marked as pending measurement.
