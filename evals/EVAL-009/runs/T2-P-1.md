# Mizan Audit: Building Retrofit Board Summary

**Bottom line:** I can't confirm all six claims. Two survive as written (3 and 5). Claims 1, 2, 4 and 6 are either overgeneralized, rest on an interested party's word, or depend on assumptions that flatter the result. Presenting them as proven would not survive a board question.

**Constraint:** I'm auditing the summary text only. I have not seen the meter data, the invoice, or the installer's documentation, so my verdicts rest on the sourcing stated in the summary.

## Per-claim assessment

**1. Energy savings across the whole portfolio.** `[Y]` `[K]` for one building only
- The evidence is real but covers one building, and that building was chosen by the facilities team because it was a showcase. Selection on the outcome is the most likely explanation for a good result here. Nothing in the summary tests the portfolio.
- Coverage: 1 building of N in the portfolio.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN** as a portfolio claim.

**2. Heat pumps are quieter than the old boilers.** `[S]`
- The only source is the installer, who is the party with a stake in the outcome. No measurement is cited, and no threshold or refutation condition exists.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.**

**3. 18% less gas per degree-day than prior winters, versus a control building at +1%.** `[K]` with open caveats
- This is the strongest claim. It uses a before/after comparison against the same building's own baseline, a concurrent matched control, degree-day weather normalization, and utility meter bills, so it is not anecdotal. Relative to the control, the difference is about 19% (0.82 / 1.01 ≈ 0.81).
- Open items that limit the claim:
  - n = 1 treated building and n = 1 control. No confidence interval is given, so the size of the effect is not quantified against noise.
  - Parallel pre-trends between the two buildings are not shown.
  - Occupancy, usage, and operating-schedule changes are not ruled out.
- It supports "this building used less gas than its control over this period." It does not yet support a causal claim about retrofits in general.
- **Verdict: PROVEN** for the building-level measurement, as stated.

**4. Tenants are more comfortable.** `[S]`
- Complaints were not tracked before the retrofit, so there is no baseline. Any comparison now would be after-only and would not measure a change.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** This cannot be shown retrospectively with the data described.

**5. Total installed cost of 2.4 million.** `[K]` with a wording fix
- The source is strong: a signed final invoice, reconciled by finance.
- The word "total" is the issue. A contractor invoice may exclude design fees, permits, owner costs, and internal labour. If those are excluded, the figure is the contract cost, not the total cost.
- **Verdict: PROVEN** for the invoiced amount. The wording should read "contract cost of 2.4M" unless finance confirms that all project costs are included.

**6. Payback under 5 years.** `[KKE]` `[Y]`
- The projection assumes gas stays at this winter's peak price. That is the most favourable assumption available, and no sensitivity case is shown.
- The summary does not give the annual savings in currency, so the payback cannot be recomputed from what is provided.
- The critical control, a payback run at average or forward gas prices, has not been done.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.**

## Coverage

6 of 6 claims were reviewed. Claims 4 and 2 cannot be verified from the material given, so those verdicts reflect missing evidence rather than evidence of falsity.

## Next steps before the board

1. **Reword claim 1** to "In the showcase building, gas use fell 18% relative to its control." Do not extend it to the portfolio unless a second building or a random sample is measured.
2. **Reword claim 5** to "contract cost 2.4M (signed, finance-reconciled)," or get finance to confirm the total.
3. **Remove claims 2 and 4** from the board summary, or label them as "installer's claim, not measured" and "not yet measured," with a plan.
4. **Rerun claim 6** at three-year average and forward gas prices, with a sensitivity table. State the gas price at which payback exceeds 5 years.
5. **Claim 3:** state the uncertainty (interval or at least the caveat about n = 1 and n = 1) and check pre-period trends. It can go to the board as written, with the scope limit stated.

## Registry seeds (Mizan registry)

These should be added to the project registry as preregistered entries, each with a refutation condition set by someone other than the installer or the team lead:

- **H-retrofit-portfolio:** Retrofitted buildings across the portfolio show ≥X% lower gas per degree-day than matched controls over the next full heating season. *Refuted if* the median effect is below the threshold or the sample of buildings is not representative.
- **H-heatpump-noise:** Measured sound level at a fixed distance is lower for heat pumps than for the old boilers. *Refuted if* the difference is not significant at a threshold agreed before measurement.
- **H-tenant-comfort:** Tenant comfort ratings, collected with a validated instrument starting now, are higher in the retrofitted building than in the control at 3 months. *Refuted if* the difference is not significant.
- **H-payback:** Simple payback is under 5 years at the 3-year average gas price. *Refuted if* the payback exceeds 5 years at that price.
