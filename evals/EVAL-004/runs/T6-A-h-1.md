# Review of Care App Board Claims

## Analysis of Each Claim

**Claim 1: The app reduces hospital readmissions by 30%**
- The comparison is between users and non-users, with a critical note that users were younger and healthier.
- This indicates selection bias and confounding variables. Age and health status are strong predictors of readmission risk, so the observed difference could be due to these factors rather than the app itself.
- Without controlling for these variables, this result is not proven.

**Claim 2: In a preregistered randomized trial (n = 1,240) with an independent data monitoring board, 90-day readmission was 11.2% with the app vs 13.9% with usual care**
- This is a rigorous methodology: randomized design eliminates selection bias, preregistration prevents p-hacking, and an independent data monitoring board provides oversight.
- This claim meets the gold standard for clinical evidence and is proven.

**Claim 3: Doctors recommend it**
- The evidence provided is that "the company's own medical advisory board endorses it."
- A company's own advisory board is not independent and represents a significant conflict of interest.
- This does not demonstrate that independent doctors broadly recommend the app.

**Claim 4: Patients love it**
- The evidence is a 4.8-star app store rating.
- App store ratings are subject to selection bias (only people motivated to leave reviews do so, and enthusiastic users are more likely to leave reviews).
- A high rating on an app store does not definitively prove that patients broadly "love it."

**Claim 5: The app has 52,000 monthly active users**
- This is a factual claim about what the analytics platform shows using standard MAU definition.
- Analytics data can be verified and audited. This is a verifiable fact.

**Claim 6: It is safe**
- The only evidence is "no adverse events were reported to the company's support email."
- Absence of reports does not equal absence of adverse events. Events could go unreported through other channels, or patients may not report them at all.
- This is not proven.
