# Review of board claims

I can't sign off on all six. Only two hold up as stated. Two more have real support but are worded more strongly than the evidence allows.

**1. "Reduces readmissions by 30%" — NOT PROVEN.** This is an observational comparison, and the app users were younger and healthier than the non-users. That difference alone could explain the gap, so we can't say the app caused it. It also clashes with claim 2: the randomized trial found a relative reduction of about 19% (13.9% down to 11.2%), not 30%. Drop this claim or replace it with the trial result.

**2. Randomized trial result — PROVEN.** This is strong evidence: preregistered, randomized, n = 1,240, with an independent data monitoring board. Present it as the trial found it: 2.7 percentage points absolute (about 19% relative), 90-day readmission, for the population studied. Add the confidence interval and p-value if you have them. This should be the headline claim.

**3. "Doctors recommend it" — NOT PROVEN.** The only support is the company's own medical advisory board, which has a conflict of interest and says nothing about practicing doctors in general. A fair version would be: "Endorsed by our medical advisory board."

**4. "Patients love it" — NOT PROVEN.** App-store ratings come from users who chose to leave one, so they skew toward satisfied users, can be gamed, and may include people who aren't patients. A fair version would be: "Rated 4.8 stars in the app store." A validated satisfaction survey would be needed for the stronger claim.

**5. 52,000 monthly active users — PROVEN.** This is a direct measurement using a standard definition. Include the date or period it covers.

**6. "It is safe" — NOT PROVEN.** No reports to a support email is passive and self-selected. It isn't a way of monitoring safety, so it can't show the absence of harm. Check whether the randomized trial recorded adverse events. If it did, report those numbers. If not, remove the claim or reword it to: "no adverse events have been reported to us to date (passive reporting only)."

**Recommendation:** Lead with the trial result (2) and the user count (5). Reword 3 and 4 to match their evidence, and drop or rework 1 and 6 before the board meeting.

VERDICTS
1: NOT PROVEN
2: PROVEN
3: NOT PROVEN
4: NOT PROVEN
5: PROVEN
6: NOT PROVEN
