# Security Product Claims Review

## Analysis

**Claim 1: Blocks 99.9% of attacks**
- Evidence: Vendor's own lab test with vendor-chosen attack set
- Issue: Vendor has conflict of interest; attack set chosen by vendor allows selection bias
- Conclusion: NOT PROVEN

**Claim 2: No breaches in four months**
- Evidence: Four months without breach detection
- Issues: Short timeframe; lacks causation (no breaches could be due to lack of attacks, not product); no breach detected ≠ no breach occurred
- Conclusion: NOT PROVEN

**Claim 3: MTTD dropped from 19 to 6 hours**
- Evidence: External SOC provider measurement over 11 months before/after; same incident definitions; no other tooling changes
- Strengths: Third-party measurement; substantial timeframe; controlled variables; change log provided
- Conclusion: PROVEN

**Claim 4: Staff find it easy to use**
- Evidence: IT team that chose it says so
- Issues: Only one biased group (they selected the product); no broader staff survey; anecdotal
- Conclusion: NOT PROVEN

**Claim 5: License costs $84,000/year**
- Evidence: Signed contract
- Conclusion: PROVEN

**Claim 6: Protects against zero-day attacks**
- Evidence: Marketing brochure
- Issues: Marketing material is promotional, not evidence; zero-days are unknowns by definition; no actual proof provided
- Conclusion: NOT PROVEN
