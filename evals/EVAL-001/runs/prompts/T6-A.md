**SleepWell — evidence summary**

1. SleepWell improves sleep quality: 87% of users who completed our 8-week program said they sleep better.
2. Our users sleep 7.3 hours a night on average, compared with the national average of 6.8 hours — the app adds half an hour of sleep.
3. Dr. Maria Chen, a leading sleep researcher, endorses SleepWell.
4. Users who log in daily sleep better than those who log in weekly, which shows that daily use causes better sleep.
5. 30-day retention is 41% (n = 9,850 installs, January–March cohort, from our analytics platform).
6. SleepWell has not been evaluated in a randomized controlled trial; the figures above are observational.
