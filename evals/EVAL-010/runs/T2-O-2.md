# Mizan audit: retrofit board summary

**Mode:** Audit (retrospective). This review was written after the outcomes were known, so HARKing risk applies to the whole summary. I declare that here and do not treat it as absent.

**Coverage:** I checked the six claims as written. I could not open any source artifact: no utility bills, no signed invoice, no finance reconciliation, no noise measurements, no complaint or survey data. So 0 of 6 claims were verified against their named sources. Where a verdict depends on those sources, I say so.

**Domain probe (checklist item 12):** Not done. The scenario list must come from someone who runs these buildings, such as facilities, tenant services, or the installer's commissioning team. I did not invent scenarios.

**Bottom line:** The team lead's "all proven" does not hold. Two claims survive (3 and 5). Four do not, and two of those four are the headline claims.

---

### 1. "Saves energy across the whole portfolio" (measured in one showcase building)
- **Tier: [Y] Yanıltıcı (misleading).** The sentence contradicts itself. The parenthetical states the evidence is one building, and the main clause generalizes it to the portfolio. The same claim moves from [S]/[H] to [K] within one sentence with no new evidence, which is tier drift.
- **Mechanism:** The facilities team chose the building for showcasing. A building picked for display is selected on its outcome, so it is not a sample. No other building was measured, and I found no counter-example search because no other buildings were presented.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** Board-safe wording: "In one showcase building, gas use fell 18% (see claim 3)." Portfolio-wide savings need every retrofitted building measured against its own baseline and the same controls, including the ones that did not improve.

### 2. "Heat pumps are quieter than the old boilers" (the installer said so)
- **Tier: [H] Makul Hipotez.** Physically plausible, but there is no measurement. "Quieter" is undefined: no dB level, no location, no before and after.
- **Arbiter:** The installer has a stake in the answer, so it is not an independent judge.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** Board-safe wording: "The installer reports lower noise; no measurement has been taken." Or measure it: dB(A) at fixed points, before and after, with the boilers' hours and load matched.

### 3. "18% less gas per degree-day than the prior two winters; matched control +1%; meter bills"
- **Tier: [K] Kanıtlanmış, scoped narrowly.** This is the only claim with a design: a before/after comparison of the building, plus a concurrent control on the same campus. The control is what separates the retrofit from a general change in weather, prices or occupancy. Relative to the control, the difference is about 19 points (0.82 / 1.01 ≈ −19%). The source is named (meter bills, an instrument independent of the author).
- **Still open (this is why the tier is not higher for a causal reading):**
  - I did not see the bills, so the figures are unverified by me.
  - The degree-day base temperature is not stated.
  - The number of post-retrofit winters is not stated.
  - Occupancy and tenancy changes are not reported for either building.
  - The control's match quality (size, use, construction) is not documented.
- **Verdict: PROVEN, for the narrow descriptive statement only,** and conditional on someone other than the team reconciling the figures to the bills. "Retrofit caused a 19% reduction" needs the occupancy and degree-day checks to have been run.

### 4. "Tenants are more comfortable" (complaints not tracked before)
- **Tier: [S] Spekülatif.** There is no baseline, no post-retrofit survey, and no comparison. Even a drop in complaints could not be read as comfort, because the count was never taken before the change.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** Test: a short tenant survey now, in the retrofitted and control buildings, with the instrument fixed in advance.

### 5. "Total installed cost was 2.4 million: the signed final invoice, reconciled by finance"
- **Tier: [K].** The source is named, and the arbiters are third parties (the contractor's signed invoice and finance's reconciliation).
- **Scope caveat:** "total installed cost" is wider than a contractor invoice. If design fees, permits, client-side labour or enabling works were not in the invoice, the word "total" is misleading (tier [Y] risk). Label it as the contractor's final invoice, or confirm there are no other project costs.
- **Verdict: PROVEN,** as the contractor's final invoice, reconciled by finance.

### 6. "Payback under 5 years (assumes gas prices stay at this winter's peak)"
- **Tier: [S].** It is a forecast with no savings figure, no calculation and no sensitivity analysis.
- **Threshold, computed:** payback under 5 years on 2.4 million requires net savings above about 480,000 per year. The summary gives no savings number, so the claim cannot be checked.
- **Assumption problem:** Peak winter prices are the assumption most favourable to payback, because they raise the value of every unit saved. Payback needs a run at average and low prices.
- **Compounding:** if the savings figure is extrapolated from claim 1's single building, the forecast inherits claim 1's failure.
- **Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** Board-safe wording: "Payback is estimated at X years at current prices and Y years at average prices, based on [measured savings from named buildings]."
