# Audit Report: Annual Security Assessment (Board Summary)

**Mode:** Audit (retrospective). I applied the core tier rules to the eight claims as written. The full reference templates and registry schema were not in my context, so this is a compact report, not the complete template. Only the summary text was provided. Appendix A and the attachments (scanner output, ticket export, IdP policy export) were not included, so I could check internal consistency but not their contents.

## Verdict

The summary's headline conclusion, that the platform has no critical vulnerabilities, is **not supported**. The document contradicts itself on scan coverage (claim 4 vs. claim 6), and three of its inferential claims (phishing, privilege escalation, and the "0 critical" conclusion) go beyond what their evidence can show. Two claims are reasonably supported as stated but need a specific control before the board relies on them.

**Coverage:** 8 of 8 claims assessed. Claims 2, 4, 6, and 7 were checkable from the text. Claims 3, 5, and 8 were assessed on their stated method only, because their attachments were not provided. Claim 1 cannot be verified from the text.

## Overall tier profile

- **Supported as stated, pending attachments:** 1 (weakly), 6 (as a count)
- **Supported only with a named control:** 3, 8
- **Refuted by the document's own data:** 4

The board summary tiers would read as mostly `[K]`. Under this audit, almost none of its conclusions are `[K]`. That gap is the main finding: the summary presents inferences (negligible phishing risk, no privilege escalation, no critical vulnerabilities) as established results.

## Recommended corrections before board circulation

1. Replace the platform-wide "no critical vulnerabilities" claim with the scoped statement from claim 2, and disclose the 5 unscanned services.
2. Remove "complete coverage" (claim 4) and state 48 of 53.
3. Reword the pen-test and phishing conclusions as bounded observations, with scope and method stated.
4. Keep the MTTP improvement, but attach the open-backlog data before presenting it as a trend.
5. Confirm the MFA result at the account level, not the policy level.
