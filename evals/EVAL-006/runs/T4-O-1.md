# Kampanya İddialar Denetimi — Kurul Sunuşu İçin

**HARKing Durumu:** Bu denetim, kampanya sonrası yapılmış retrospektif bir analizdir. Denetim sırasında sonuçlar görülmüş; iddiaların tümü kampanyadan sonra formüle edilmiştir.

---

## Denetim Bulguları

### 1. Kampanya satışları artırdı

**Claim:** Kampanya satışları artırdı.

**Evidence provided:** Kampanya ayında satışlar arttı; aynı ay yılın en yoğun bayram dönemiydi.

**Analysis:**
- The claim observes a sales increase in the campaign month
- A confound exists: that month was the year's busiest holiday season
- No control group; no pre-registered analysis
- The sales increase is explained by EITHER the campaign OR the holiday, but not identified separately
- The evidence only establishes that sales rose; it does not prove the campaign caused it

**Tier:** `[KKE]` / Critical control missing
The mechanism cannot be isolated from seasonal effects without a matched control group.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### 2. Marka bilinirliği yükseldi

**Claim:** Brand awareness increased.

**Evidence provided:** Ajansın kendi raporu; ölçüm yöntemi belirtilmemiş.

**Analysis:**
- Source: the agency's own report (internal, biased incentive)
- Measurement method is not specified — the instrument is unknown
- No baseline stated; no control group
- No way to verify the claim without knowing what was measured
- Agency bias: they benefit from showing positive results

**Tier:** `[H]` / Plausible hypothesis
Theoretically grounded but empirical support is absent and measurement is opaque.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### 3. Rastgele seçilen iller üzerinde kontrollü deney

**Claim:** 20 randomly selected provinces received the campaign; 20 matched control provinces did not. With pre-registered analysis and independent analytics team measurement, campaign provinces showed a 4.1% higher sales increase than control provinces (GA 95% CI: 2.0–6.2%).

**Evidence provided:** 
- Random assignment
- Matched control group
- Pre-registered analysis (HARKing guard)
- Independent third-party measurement (analytics team)
- Numeric result with confidence interval

**Analysis:**
- Random assignment protects against selection bias
- Control group isolates campaign effect from seasonal/market effects
- Pre-registered threshold locks analysis before seeing results
- Independent measurement rules out agency bias
- Confidence interval supports the result (does not include zero)
- Threshold is numeric and measurable

**Tier:** `[K]` / Proven
Direct evidence supports the claim; source cited; threshold met; mechanism identified.

**Verdict:** PROVEN

---

### 4. Kampanya bütçesi 3,6 milyon TL oldu

**Claim:** Campaign budget was 3.6 million TL.

**Evidence provided:** Accounting records and media agency paid invoices; reconciled by internal audit.

**Analysis:**
- Objective fact (budget amount is verifiable)
- Multiple independent sources (accounting + agency invoices)
- Third-party verification (internal audit reconciliation)
- Audit leaves no room for interpretation

**Tier:** `[K]` / Proven
Direct evidence supports it; source cited; threshold met.

**Verdict:** PROVEN

---

### 5. Müşteriler reklamı sevdi

**Claim:** Customers loved the advertisement.

**Evidence provided:** 15 selected positive comments on social media.

**Analysis:**
- "Selected" = curated, not representative sampling
- 15 comments is a very small sample; no total comment count provided
- Selection bias: showing only positive comments, not the full distribution
- No baseline: is 15 positive comments high or low for this platform/post?
- The word "loved" is interpretation; 15 positive comments support "some people liked it," not "customers loved it"
- Framing as evidence of universal approval when it is curated approval

**Tier:** `[Y]` / Misleading
Technically contains truth (some positive comments exist) but frames them to imply broader support than the evidence justifies.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN

---

### 6. Kampanya kalıcı bir müşteri tabanı yarattı

**Claim:** Campaign created a permanent customer base.

**Evidence provided:** 3 weeks have passed since the campaign.

**Analysis:**
- "Permanent" means lasting indefinitely
- 3 weeks is insufficient time to determine permanence
- Could measure: repeat purchase rate, retention rate, churn rate — but only at >1 year horizon
- Current data shows: nothing. A retention claim requires follow-up data
- This is a forecast about future behavior disguised as a present fact

**Tier:** `[S]` / Speculative
Interesting; not currently testable with available data. Measurable only with multi-month follow-up.

**Verdict:** NOT PROVEN
