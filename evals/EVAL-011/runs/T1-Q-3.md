## Notes on the two that survive

**Claim 1.** The number is a direct measurement of the full population. Before the board sees it, confirm two things with the data platform team:
- Whether automated acknowledgements count as "first response." If they do, 40s measures the bot's auto-reply, not a substantive answer.
- Whether ticket volume or staffing changed across the 20 weeks. The hiring freeze could alter human response times in the "before" and "after" windows.

Present it as "time to first response fell," not "the bot caused it." The summary does not make a causal claim, but the board will read one into it.

**Claim 4.** The reported CI is narrower than the sample size supports. For about 2,500 customers per arm and a roughly 10% rate, a 95% CI for the difference should be about ±1.7 points, or roughly −4.0 to −0.6. The reported ±0.7 implies around 14,000 per arm. Either the n is different from 5,000, or the CI was computed on a different unit. Ask analytics for n per arm and the CI method. Even at the wider interval, the effect excludes zero, so the conclusion survives, but the precision figure should not go on a slide until it is checked.

## Suggested board wording

- **1 (keep):** "Median time to first response fell from 4.2 hours to 40 seconds over the 10 weeks after launch (all 38,000 tickets)." Add the definition of "first response" once confirmed.
- **2 (reword or drop):** "Early transcript review suggests positive reception; no preference comparison has been run."
- **3 (reword):** "The vendor dashboard counts chat-window closures as resolutions. Independent resolution has not yet been measured."
- **4 (keep, after the n check):** "In a randomized test (5,000 customers, 6 weeks), bot-first routing reduced 7-day repeat contacts from 11.4% to 9.1%."
- **5 (drop or relabel):** "Vendor projection, not yet tested."
- **6 (drop):** No measurement exists.

## Next steps, in priority order

1. **Analytics team:** send the n per arm and CI method for claim 4. This is a one-line question and it decides whether the precision figure can be shown.
2. **Data platform team:** confirm the "first response" definition for claim 1, and check for volume or staffing changes across the window.
3. **Vendor:** ask for reopen rate within 7 days, escalation-to-human rate, and CSAT, so "resolved" can be measured by something other than window closure.
4. **Agents (claim 6):** run a short pulse survey, or compare attrition with the same quarter last year or with a team that did not face the freeze. Do not state happiness until one of these exists.
5. **Missing card:** the summary shows nothing about failures. Before the board meets, ask the support lead which ticket types the bot cannot handle (for example billing disputes, outages, non-English tickets). Those answers are the evidence this deck is missing, and I cannot supply them myself.
