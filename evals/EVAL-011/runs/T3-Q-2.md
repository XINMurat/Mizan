## Counter-example sweep

## Conjunctions the summary cannot show
- **Speed × quality (claims 1 and 3):** A tool that cuts screening effort can shorten time-to-hire while lowering quality. Claim 4 cannot rule this out because it measures shortlist agreement, not post-hire results.
- **Speed × bias (claims 2 and 3):** If the tool carries bias, faster screening applies that filter to more candidates sooner. Nothing in the summary tests this pair.

## Missing card
The summary shows adopter enthusiasm and one favourable number. It has no card for rejected candidates who were later hired elsewhere, requisitions closed without a hire, recruiter overrides, or appeals. Those would be the failures a board most needs to see.
